## Data
- **100% synthetic.** Wallet seed written by the author's assistant from docs/11; test requests generated by Gemini 3.8 Flash (`agy`) from the seed names and docs/11 categories.
- **No human labelled the test set.** Labels come from three LLM families (agy, Fable, Haiku) labelling independently against the seed, merged by `eval/gen/adjudicate.py` (2 of 3 wins; no majority = the safest state). Agreement and every adjudicated row are in `eval/data/`. Policy calls made by the adjudicator are listed in `eval/data/README.md` (one was applied after the first dev run). **No native-speaker review has happened yet:** reported numbers are against LLM-adjudicated labels, not human ground truth.
- Voice subset: **none recorded yet.** When collected: consenting speakers, fictional content, local only (eval/voice/README.md).
- Changes after the single held-out run: resolver fix for a bare «أبو» (2026-10-17) and the voice path. The held-out number describes commit 1445bff.
- No real customer data. No real payment rails. Mock wallet only.
- Free-tier providers may train on prompts (Google, Mistral say so) → only synthetic data was sent.
- Held-out set SHA-256: `13e0fa33f989210fa1e2323c7cfdf020f1b6baae4ec0b78b4a9552aef9faf4a2` (frozen 2026-10-16 before any held-out run; `eval/data/heldout.sha256`).
